# 🎬 Videos Folder – Yogi Stunt School

Drop your video files here. The website will automatically pick them up.

## Required Files

| Filename        | Used In             | Recommended Format |
|-----------------|---------------------|--------------------|
| `hero-reel.mp4` | Hero background     | H.264 MP4, muted autoplay-safe |
| `reel-1.mp4`    | Showreel – video 1  | H.264 MP4          |
| `reel-2.mp4`    | Showreel – video 2  | H.264 MP4          |
| `reel-3.mp4`    | Showreel – video 3  | H.264 MP4          |

## Tips
- Convert to H.264 MP4 for best browser compatibility
- Hero background video: keep under 20MB, 720p is fine (auto-muted)
- Showreel videos: 1080p recommended
- Use HandBrake (free) to compress videos before uploading
- Keep filenames exactly as listed above
